03 / WHAT MAKES THIS AN AGENT

Don't generate.
Diagnose. Repair. Verify.

Live mode will resolve real lineup identities with Qloo, rank a fixed comparison universe and propose repairs only when evidence is complete.

01Diagnose the gap

Locate which audience is least served when an act is removed.

02Constrain the repair

Preserve the remaining program. Require organizer-confirmed alternatives.

03Show a checkable counterfactual

Display identical-entity rank comparisons before and after the single change, or refuse the repair.

This offline simulation demonstrates the decision policy only. Percentile ranks are not audience satisfaction probabilities. Qloo does not verify artist availability, contracts, prices, travel or performance rights.
